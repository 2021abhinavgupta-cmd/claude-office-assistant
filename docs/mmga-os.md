# MMGA OS (source: "MMGA OS V2.pdf", loaded 2026-10-12)

This is the company operating system as written by the founders. Some passages
in the source are written as proposals ("I would...", "I'd add..."); they are
marked (proposal) here. Treat this as the current plan, not a rulebook that
has been confirmed point by point.

Shorthand: KG = Kshitij (founder). VG = Vidit (founder). DM = the person who
owns a project/client. SMM = social media management. 360 = full-service client.

## Level 1: Roles

### SL1 Leadership
- **KG, Business:** sales, revenue, expansion, partnerships, commercial
  decisions. For content he supplies big campaign ideas, brand positioning,
  business context and client/business understanding.
- **VG, Operations + Strategy + Quality:** creative direction, operations,
  quality standards, client satisfaction, major decisions, new processes,
  AI and automation, team development, training, escalations. VG is the
  final internal quality gate. VG should teach people how to make decisions,
  not make every decision for them. (Source note: VG still needs to improve
  creative output, especially content, management of things, and adoption
  of AI.)

### SL2 Project ownership ("Captains")
Every project/client has a DM. The DM owns getting it delivered properly and
on time: task allocation, timelines, content coordination, internal feedback,
client feedback and communication, execution priorities, project status,
making sure the team has what it needs, and deciding what is ready for VG.

**DM owns the project. VG owns the standard.**

### SL3 Execution
Content, Design, Video, Dev/Automation. They own execution, basic creative
choices, finding references, finding new AI/tools, minor corrections and
solving problems independently. They are responsible for thinking, not just
taking instructions.

### One responsibility per level
Team: "Own your work." DM: "Own your project." VG: "Own the standard."
KG: "Own the business." Goal: everyone owns something and everyone learns
something.

## Decision flow
Team member: can I solve this myself? Yes: solve it. No: can I find 2
possible solutions? Yes: take it to the DM, the DM decides. Needs a
strategic or major decision: VG. This stops VG from being the default answer
to everything.

**MMGA problem-solving rule:** don't bring a problem without trying to solve
it first. Format: Problem (what is happening) / Tried (what I already tried) /
Options (possible solutions) / Recommendation (what I think we should do) /
Decision (what we finally do). A junior may say: "I don't know the solution
yet, but I found these two possible directions."

## Operating rhythm
- **Monday 4 PM, Weekly Planning.** Everyone prepares before 4 PM. Go client
  by client, not person by person (content, design, video, performance,
  shoot for each client). Assign tasks, owners, deadlines; identify blockers
  and dependencies. DM updates final decisions.
- **Daily 6 PM, Quality + Approval** (not a general meeting). Nothing unclear
  or unfinished moves forward. Content team approves week-2 content;
  production team approves next week's content; plus anything needing VG's
  decision. Resolve blockers and revisions; convert feedback into tasks.
- **Wednesday 2 PM, Team lunch and bonding**, 1 h 30 min: lunch, then
  games/team-building.
- **Friday, Weekly Closing/Review.** What was finished, what wasn't, why,
  what is urgent, what is blocked, what is at risk; confirm next week's
  priorities. Friday 6 PM final checkpoint: what's ready for next week,
  what's not, why, what needs urgent attention, will anything affect client
  delivery.
- **7:30 PM, Daily Closing.** Lumina checks task activity (missing updates,
  blockers, tomorrow's priorities), asks each person: what did you
  complete / what's pending / what's blocked, records it, and creates a
  short management summary.
- **Weekend rule (proposal):** weekend is only for critical backlog that can
  affect a client commitment or Monday delivery. Friday makes backlog
  visible; it must not push it to the weekend automatically.
- Flow: Monday planning, Mon-Fri execution, daily 6 PM approval, Friday
  closing (done / pending / urgent / blocked), Monday new planning.

## SOP rules
1. Own your work. 2. Don't bring a problem without thinking. 3. Don't start
unclear work. 4. Every task has one owner. 5. Never silently get blocked.
6. Client feedback becomes a task.

**Two operating types.** Recurring services (fixed SOP): social media,
performance marketing, regular content production, recurring campaigns.
Project-based services (timeline + tasks + ownership + deadlines +
follow-up, no rigid SOP): branding, website, website changes, brochures,
one-off videos, other client projects. For project work Lumina's job is:
create project, create timeline, assign tasks, track progress, identify
blockers, follow up, close tasks.

## SMM SOP: the 3-week rolling cycle
Content is planned 2 weeks ahead, produced 1 week ahead, published in the
current week. Every week the pipeline moves forward: while week 1 publishes,
week 2 is produced and week 3 is planned. Example (Monday 5 Oct): publish =
week of 5 Oct, production = week of 12 Oct, content planning = week of
19 Oct.

At Monday's meeting each recurring SM client gets: (1) what is going live
this week (already produced), (2) what is being produced this week (next
week's content), (3) what is being planned this week (content two weeks out).
Every item has Owner + Format + Deadline + Status.

- **Content plan (content of the 3rd week):** Mon planning and ideation, Tue
  content development, Wed content lock, Thu content finalisation, Fri
  production handoff.
- **Production plan (content of the 2nd week):** Mon tasks distributed, Tue
  execution, Wed first review, Thu changes, Fri final approval.
